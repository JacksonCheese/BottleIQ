# Roadmap

## Next five priorities

1. **Pilot intake and import reconciliation:** test exports from 3–5 actual store systems, reconcile row counts/revenue/units, add import preview totals, coverage gaps, returns, and safe correction/undo tooling. Prevent silent data completeness assumptions.
2. **Secure hosted onboarding:** managed authentication with verified email/recovery, organization invitations, shared throttling, HTTPS deployment, monitoring, encrypted backups and restore drills, retention/deletion, and mutation audit history.
3. **Reliable replenishment state:** link reviewed drafts to placed orders and incoming records; expand receiving with shortage/return corrections, freshness enforcement matched to each store, vendor case packs/minimums, allocation constraints, and budget review.
4. **Evidence of ROI:** capture owner overrides and actual receipts; compare purchases, stockouts, cash tied up, and gross profit against a pre-pilot baseline. Never claim savings from recommendations alone.
5. **Scale and model validation:** durable import jobs, incremental aggregates, server-side pagination, realistic load tests, backtesting, launch dates, stockout-censored demand, promotion and seasonal adjustments.

## Later, after pilot evidence

- POS adapters/integrations for Square, Clover, Lightspeed, Korona, and others.
- Distributor catalog integrations, negotiated costs, pack availability, and electronic purchase-order workflows.
- Explainable forecasting, promotional demand detection, broader anomaly detection.
- Multi-store inventory transfers and chain purchasing coordination.
- Pricing intelligence and an AI natural-language analyst over permission-scoped metrics.

Automatic supplier submission requires explicit review/approval design. Neural networks, chatbots, accounting, payments, native mobile apps, employee scheduling, consumer features, and marketplaces are not part of this MVP.
